Why Revnuvo exists.

The web was built around human attention. A person could inspect a page, recognize a brand, decide whether a request felt legitimate, and pause when something seemed wrong.

Autonomous systems do not have that luxury. They need trust signals that are explicit, composable, payable, and available before an action is taken. They need infrastructure that respects uncertainty without freezing every workflow in place.

Revnuvo exists because the next generation of software will not only retrieve information. It will make decisions. Trust cannot remain trapped in dashboards, policies, and human memory. It has to become programmable.
